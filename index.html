Frequently Asked Questions

Is the certificate an official diploma or degree?

No. ORIZIS Academy issues a Certificate of Completion that proves you finished a course and passed its exam. It is not a government- or TEVETA-accredited qualification, diploma or degree. It is real proof of the skills you learned, useful to show employers and clients.

Do I need a computer or a credit card?

No. You can do everything on a smartphone, and you pay with Mobile Money — MTN MoMo, Airtel Money or Zamtel. No bank account or credit card is needed.

How long does a course take?

Each course is designed to take about one hour, split into short lessons. You can start, stop and continue whenever it suits you — your progress is saved.

What score do I need to pass?

After you finish all the lessons you take a short exam. You need 70% or more to pass and receive your certificate. If you don't pass, you can review the lessons and try again.

Can an employer check my certificate?

Yes. Every certificate has a unique verification code and a link. Anyone can open the verification page, enter the code, and confirm your name, course and date.

What is the ORIZIS Builder Network?

It's an opportunity for our best web-building graduates. After completing a Builder-track course and building a portfolio project, you can apply to join a pool of vetted builders who may be offered real paid project work. It is an opportunity to apply — not a guaranteed job.
